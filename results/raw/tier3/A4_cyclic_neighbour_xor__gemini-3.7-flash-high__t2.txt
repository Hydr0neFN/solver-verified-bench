```
Rule (2D toroidal cellular XOR):

┌──────────────┬──────────────┐
│  OUT[r, c]   │ IN[r, c+1]   │ ← Right neighbor (mod 4)
├──────────────┼──────────────┤
│ IN[r+1, c]   │              │
└──────────────┴──────────────┘
       ▲
       └─ Down neighbor (mod 4)

OUT[r, c] = IN[(r + 1) % 4, c] ⊕ IN[r, (c + 1) % 4]
```

- Each cell `OUT[r, c]` is computed as the XOR sum of its neighbor below `(r+1, c)` and its neighbor to the right `(r, c+1)` with periodic boundary wrap (mod 4).
