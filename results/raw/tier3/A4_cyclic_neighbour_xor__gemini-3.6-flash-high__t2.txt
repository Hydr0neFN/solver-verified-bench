Each cell in the output grid at row $r$ and column $c$ (with periodic wrapping modulo 4) is obtained by taking the XOR (exclusive OR) of its down neighbor and its right neighbor in the input grid:

$$\text{OUT}[r, c] = \text{IN}[(r+1) \bmod 4, c] \oplus \text{IN}[r, (c+1) \bmod 4]$$
