[IN(r, c+1)] ──┐

Each cell $(r, c)$ in `OUT` is the XOR of its right neighbor and bottom neighbor with periodic/toroidal boundary conditions:
$$\text{OUT}[r, c] = \text{IN}[r, (c + 1) \bmod 4] \oplus \text{IN}[(r + 1) \bmod 4, c]$$

Applying to Query:
- Row 0: `(0^1, 0^1, 1^0, 1^0)` $\to$ `1 1 1 1`
