```
Timeline of Network Partition & Lease Expiration:

Time (s)     Worker 1                    Scheduler                    Worker 2
  0s         [Executing Job J] ---------> [Lease Active]
 15s         ! Partitioned (R5)          
             [Still Executing J]         [Awaiting Heartbeat...]
 30s         [Still Executing J]         ! Lease Expires (R3)
                                         │
                                         ▼ (R4: MUST Reassign)
                                         [Assigns J to Worker 2] ---> [Executing Job J]
 >30s        [Executing Job J] <====================================> [Executing Job J]
                                    ! CONCURRENT EXECUTION (Violates R11)
```

- **R4** (Mandatory Reassignment) and **R11** (No Concurrent Execution) are mutually unsatisfiable.
- Under **R5**, a worker partitioned while executing a job cannot detect the partition and continues running.
- When Worker 1 is partitioned, heartbeats fail and its lease expires after 30 seconds (**R3**).
- **R4** mandates that the scheduler **must** reassign the job to Worker 2 upon lease expiry.
- Because Worker 1 is still running, both Worker 1 and Worker 2 execute the job simultaneously, violating **R11**.
- Enforcing **R11** requires withholding reassignment (violating **R4**), while enforcing **R4** forces concurrent execution (violating **R11**).

FINAL: R4 and R11
