    ▼                     ▼
[Col Parity Mask: M] ──> [R ⊕ M] ──> [Output]

- Rotate grid 90° clockwise: `R[r][c] = IN[3-c][r]`
- Compute column parity vector $M$: `M[c] = (sum_{r=0..3} IN[r][c]) mod 2`
- Invert column $c$ if parity is odd: `OUT[r][c] = R[r][c] ^ M[c]`
