```
[W1 Leased] ──► [W1 Executes J] ────► [Lease Expires] ───► [W2 Executes J]
                 │ (Run #1)             │ (Partition/Drop)   │ (Run #2)
                 └──────────────────────┴────────────────────┼────────────────►
                                                             ▼
                                                Total Executions = 2
                                                ! Violates R2 (at most once)
                                                ← Forced by R4 (MUST reassign)
```

- **R2 (At-most-once execution)** requires $\le 1$ total execution across all workers.
- **R4 (Mandatory reassignment)** forces the scheduler to reassign whenever a lease expires.
- If Worker 1 executes the job but network delay/partition prevents heartbeat/ACK delivery, Worker 1's lease expires at the scheduler.
- R4 unconditionally forces reassignment to Worker 2, producing a second execution ($\ge 2$), directly contradicting R2.
- Bounded clock skew (R10) prevents concurrent execution (R11), but cannot prevent duplicate sequential execution mandated by R4.
