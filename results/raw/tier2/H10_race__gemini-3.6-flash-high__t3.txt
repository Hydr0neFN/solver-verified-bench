```
   ├── SELECT sent_at ───────┼──────────────────────────>│ (Returns None)
   │                          ├── SELECT sent_at ────────>│ (Returns None)
   ├── mailer.send() [!]      │                           │
   │   (Email #1 sent)        │                           │
   │                          ├── mailer.send() [!]       │
   │                          │   (Email #2 sent)         │
   v                          v                           v
```

Reasoning:
- Under READ COMMITTED isolation, `SELECT sent_at` does not acquire a lock on the missing row.
- Two concurrent execution paths both execute `SELECT`, see no existing row (`row is None`), and bypass the guard check.
- Both callers invoke `mailer.send()` before attempting the `INSERT`, causing the welcome email to be dispatched twice.

FINAL: Check-then-act race condition
