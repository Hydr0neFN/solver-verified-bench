```
Time (s)    0               20              30              40
────────────┼───────────────┼───────────────┼───────────────┼────────►
Worker 1    [=== Leased ===] [== Partition ==] [=== Still Running ===]
Scheduler   [ Lease active ] [ Awaiting HB  ] [ Lease expired ]
Worker 2                                    [=== Reassigned & Running ===]
                                            ▲

```

- Under R4, scheduler MUST reassign job to a new worker immediately upon lease expiration (t = 30s).
- Under R5, Worker 1 may be partitioned while still executing and cannot detect the partition, so it continues execution past lease expiry.
- Reassignment forces Worker 2 to execute concurrently with Worker 1, directly violating R11 (no concurrent execution).

FINAL: R4 and R11
