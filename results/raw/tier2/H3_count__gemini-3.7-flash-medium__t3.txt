- Forbidden positions form a tridiagonal board: $p(i) \in \{i-1, i, i+1\}$ forbidden for each $i \in \{1, \dots, n\}$.
- Equivalent to placing $n$ non-attacking rooks avoiding the tridiagonal band.
- Computed via dynamic programming over subsets of assigned values row-by-row (OEIS A001883).
