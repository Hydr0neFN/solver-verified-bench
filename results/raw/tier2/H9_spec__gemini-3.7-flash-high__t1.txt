```
                              │
                  (Violates R2: At-Most-Once)
```

- **R4** enforces at-least-once execution to maximize task completion utility by mandating reassignment upon lease expiration.
- **R2** strictly mandates at-most-once execution ($N \le 1$) to eliminate the negative utility of duplicated side effects.
- If a worker's lease expires after execution has started or completed, **R4** forces a second worker to execute the same job ($N \ge 2$), creating a direct logical contradiction with **R2**.
