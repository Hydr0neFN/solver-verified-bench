```
Requirement Evaluation Matrix:
│ Requirement                   │ Status                      │
├───────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┤
│ R11 (No Concurrency)          │ [x] Satisfied (Sequential)  │
│ R2  (At-Most-Once)            │ ! Violated (Executed Twice) │
```

- **R2** guarantees **at-most-once** execution (a job is executed $\le 1$ time).
- **R4** mandates job reassignment upon lease expiry, forcing **at-least-once** re-execution semantics.
- If Worker 1 starts executing a job and its lease expires, R4 requires the scheduler to reassign the job to Worker 2, resulting in multiple execution attempts for the same job.
- Thus, R2 and R4 represent mutually unsatisfiable execution semantics.
