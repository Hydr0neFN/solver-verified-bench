Rule: Integers with exactly 3 set bits (Hamming weight = 3) in increasing order.

- Terms 1–20 cover 3-bit to 6-bit numbers: $\binom{2}{2} + \binom{3}{2} + \binom{4}{2} + \binom{5}{2} = 1 + 3 + 6 + 10 = 20$.
- Term 25 is the 5th number in the 7-bit group ($2^6 + 2^a + 2^b$ where $0 \le b < a < 6$):
  - T21: $64 + 2 + 1 = 67$
  - T22: $64 + 4 + 1 = 69$
  - T23: $64 + 4 + 2 = 70$
  - T24: $64 + 8 + 1 = 73$
  - T25: $64 + 8 + 2 = 74$
