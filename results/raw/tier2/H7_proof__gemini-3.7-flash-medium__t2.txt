```
[1..5: x > 0] ──> x + 1/x >= 2
                      │
                      ▼

```

- Step 1 assumes x > 0.
- Step 5 establishes x + 1/x >= 2 only under the assumption x > 0.
- Step 6 generalizes to all real x, which does not follow (and is false for x <= 0).
