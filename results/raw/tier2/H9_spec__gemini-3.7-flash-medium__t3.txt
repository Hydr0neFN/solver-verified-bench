```
[Worker 1 executes job] ──> [Lease expires] ──> [Scheduler reassigns (R4)]
                                                         │
                                                         ▼
                                                [Worker 2 executes job]
                                                         │
                                                         ▼
                                            Total executions >= 2  ! (violates R2)
```

- R2 mandates execution count <= 1 under all conditions.
- R4 requires mandatory reassignment whenever a worker's lease expires.
- If Worker 1 executes the job (or performs irreversible side effects) and its lease expires before completion or acknowledgment, R4 forces the scheduler to reassign the job to Worker 2.
- Worker 2 executes the job, resulting in >= 2 executions and directly violating R2.
