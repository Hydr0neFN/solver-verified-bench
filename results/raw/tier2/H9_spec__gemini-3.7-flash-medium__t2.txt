```
[Job Running on Worker W1] ──(Operator cancels)──> [Job Cancelled (R12)]
            │                                                │
     (Lease expires)                                         │ (Contradiction)
            ▼                                                │
  [Scheduler MUST reassign (R4)] ◄───────────────────────────┘
```

- R12 permits an operator to cancel an active job at any time prior to reaching a terminal state.
- Once cancelled, the job is terminal and must not be scheduled or executed further.
- Worker W1 ceases heartbeating (or partition/timeout occurs), causing W1's 30-second lease to expire.
- R4 unconditionally mandates that whenever a worker's lease expires, the scheduler **MUST** reassign the job to another worker.
- Satisfying R4 forces reassignment and execution of the cancelled job, directly violating R12; honoring R12 prevents reassignment, directly violating R4.

FINAL: R4 and R12
