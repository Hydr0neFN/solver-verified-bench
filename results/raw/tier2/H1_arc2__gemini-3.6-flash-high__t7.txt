### Reasoning

1. **Deterministic Rule**:
   - **Step 1**: Rotate the input binary grid 90° clockwise.
   - **Step 2**: Calculate the column parity (sum modulo 2) for each column of the *original* input grid. For any column with odd parity (sum mod 2 = 1), invert all bits in that column of the rotated grid.

2. **Query Execution**:
   - **Input Grid**:

