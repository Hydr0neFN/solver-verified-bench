R4 requires the scheduler to reassign a job as soon as its lease expires, even if the original worker is still executing it (possible under R5’s partition scenario).  
R11 forbids any job from ever being executed concurrently by two workers.  
When a partition causes the lease to expire while the original worker continues, R4 forces a second worker to start the same job, violating R11. Hence these two requirements cannot both be satisfied.

FINAL: R4 and R11
