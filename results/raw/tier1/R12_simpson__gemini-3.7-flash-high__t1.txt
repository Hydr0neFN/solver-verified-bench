```
└─────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┴───────────┘
* Confounded aggregate (Simpson's paradox).
```

- **Causal adjustment**: Stone size is a confounder. Conditioned on stone size, Treatment A has a higher success rate in both subgroups (93.10% vs 86.67% small; 73.00% vs 68.75% large) → prefer **A**.
- **Aggregate calculation for A**: $(81 + 192) / (87 + 263) = 273 / 350 = 0.7800 \to 78.00\%$.

FINAL: prefer=A, A_overall=78.00%
