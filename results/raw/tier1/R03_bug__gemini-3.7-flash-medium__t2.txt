```
Thread 1: locks 'A' ──────> waits for 'B' ──────┐
                                                ▼
Thread 2: locks 'B' ──────> waits for 'A' ──> [ DEADLOCK ] !
```

- Locks in `self._locks` are acquired in arbitrary iteration order of `items`.
- Concurrent threads requesting overlapping SKUs in differing orders cause circular wait.
- Fix: sort keys canonically (e.g. `sorted(items)`) before calling `.acquire()`.

FINAL: Deadlock due to inconsistent lock acquisition order
