Bayes' Theorem calculation:
- `P(Disease)` = 0.001
- `P(No Disease)` = 0.999
- `P(Positive | Disease)` = 0.99
- `P(Positive | No Disease)` = 0.05

`P(Disease | Positive)` = `(0.99 * 0.001) / ((0.99 * 0.001) + (0.05 * 0.999))`
= `0.00099 / (0.00099 + 0.04995)`
= `0.00099 / 0.05094`
= `99 / 5094` ≈ `1.94%`

FINAL: 1.94%
