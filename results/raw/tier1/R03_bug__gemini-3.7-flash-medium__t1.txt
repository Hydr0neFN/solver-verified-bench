```
Thread 1 (items: [A, B])            Thread 2 (items: [B, A])
       │                                   │
       ▼                                   ▼
Acquires Lock(A)                    Acquires Lock(B)
       │                                   │
       ▼                                   ▼
Blocks on Lock(B) ────────────────► Holds Lock(B)
       ▲                                   │
       │                                   ▼
Holds Lock(A) ◄──────────────────── Blocks on Lock(A)
       │                                   │
       └────────────── ! DEADLOCK ─────────┘
```

- Dict iteration order follows caller key insertion order.
- Locks are acquired sequentially via `for sku in items: self._locks[sku].acquire()` without a consistent canonical ordering (e.g. `sorted(items)`).
- Concurrent threads reserving overlapping SKUs in different key orders produce circular wait → permanent deadlock.
