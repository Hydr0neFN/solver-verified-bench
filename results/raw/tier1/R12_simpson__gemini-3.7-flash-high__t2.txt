- Stratifying by stone size resolves Simpson's paradox: Treatment A yields higher success rates in both small (93.10% > 86.67%) and large (73.00% > 68.75%) subgroups → patient should prefer **A**.
- Treatment A aggregate success rate: $\frac{81 + 192}{87 + 263} = \frac{273}{350} = 78.00\%$.
