- Controlling for confounder: Treatment A outperforms Treatment B across both subgroups (small: 93.10% vs 86.67%, large: 73.00% vs 68.75%).
- Aggregation reversal caused by disproportionate assignment of severe cases (large stones) to Treatment A (263/350 = 75.14%) vs Treatment B (80/350 = 22.86%).
- Preference: Patient should prefer Treatment A regardless of stone size.
