```
[Thread 1: holds A] ──waits for B──> [Thread 2: holds B]
         ▲                                   │
         └─────────────waits for A───────────┘ !
```

Locks are acquired in the iteration order of `items`. When concurrent callers request overlapping items in different orderings (e.g., `{'A': 1, 'B': 1}` vs `{'B': 1, 'A': 1}`), circular lock dependencies occur. 

Normalizing lock acquisition sequence (e.g. `sorted(items)`) is required to prevent deadlocks.
