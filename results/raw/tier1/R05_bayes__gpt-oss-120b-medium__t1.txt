The disease prevalence is 0.1 % (1 / 1000).  
True‑positive rate = 99 % → P(positive | disease)=0.99.  
False‑positive rate = 5 % (1 − specificity) → P(positive | no disease)=0.05.

Posterior probability:
\[
P(\text{disease}\mid\text{positive})=\frac{0.001\times0.99}{0.001\times0.99+0.999\times0.05}
=\frac{0.00099}{0.00099+0.04995}
=\frac{0.00099}{0.05094}\approx0.01945.
\]

Convert to percentage → 1.945 % → 1.95 % (two decimal places).

FINAL: 1.95%
