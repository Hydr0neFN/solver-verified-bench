```
┌───────────────────────────┐         ┌───────────────────────────┐
│     R1: Immutable Log     │         │   R3: Right to Erasure    │
│ Append-only; no deletion  │ ◄─ ! ─► │ Produce state with no     │
│ or modification permitted │         │ trace of transaction      │
└───────────────────────────┘         └───────────────────────────┘
```

- R1 mandates strict append-only persistence (rows may never be updated or deleted).
- R3 mandates complete removal of records ("no trace that a voided transaction ever existed").
- Removing all traces of a record requires deleting/modifying rows, directly violating R1.
